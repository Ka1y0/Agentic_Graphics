# Labs

Labs is the research-facing edge of Agentic_Graphics. It records directions that may produce experiments, measurements, or future adapters without presenting them as implemented Core features.

Research directions include:

- non-photorealistic rendering (NPR);
- pixel, toon, cel, and outline reconstruction;
- hybrid 2D/3D rendering;
- WebGL and WebGPU graphics experiments;
- stylized simulation;
- agentic scene construction.

Pixel Harbor already explores several of these ideas inside its own Three.js runtime. General-purpose Three.js NPR adapters, a universal 2.5D runtime, and generic world generation remain research or planned work.
